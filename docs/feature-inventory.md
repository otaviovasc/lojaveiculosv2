# Feature Inventory

> This is a technical surface inventory, not a statement that a feature is
> commercially safe, adopted by customers, compliant, supportable, or ready for
> V1 cutover. Commercial launch is gated separately below.

Every migrated feature needs one row before implementation. Status values:

- `Production-ready`: runtime route/UI exists, real adapters are wired, and the
  remaining setup is environment/provider configuration.
- `Runtime-ready`: route/UI exists and production adapters are wired for owned
  data, but one external provider is not enabled until credentials/endpoints are
  supplied.
- `Placeholder`: visible navigation exists, but the feature is intentionally not
  implemented yet.
- `Migration-deferred`: product surface exists, but historical V1 migration is
  intentionally skipped for now.
- `Critical gap`: the capability blocks safe commercialization, retention, or
  migration and must be resolved before adding speculative product breadth.

### CRM messaging provider operating contract

- Target segment: independent dealerships using ZAPI today or adopting
  official WhatsApp and Instagram professional-account messaging.
- Customer outcome: receive and respond to supported customer conversations
  from one store-scoped CRM inbox without losing provider identity or showing a
  false delivery result.
- Leading metrics: provider send-acceptance and delivery rates by provider,
  inbound-to-first-response latency, duplicate-event rate, and visible
  provider-failure rate.
- Billing and entitlement: access remains under the existing `crm`
  entitlement. Official-provider allowance, variable cost, quota, and
  pass-through policy require commercial approval before customer enablement.
- Support owner: CRM engineering owns service and adapter correctness; platform
  operations owns provider credentials, webhook health, monitoring, and
  incident response.
- Failure and degraded state: the selected provider fails closed and remains
  visible as unavailable or failed. Official provider errors never fall back to
  ZAPI, unsupported operations never claim that a message was sent, and
  ambiguous timeouts or 5xx responses are not automatically replayed. Only
  explicit 429 responses receive bounded retry.
- Current readiness: this is an implemented runtime slice, not evidence of
  commercial readiness. Official free-form conversation start is blocked;
  approved WhatsApp template initiation and the 24-hour customer-service window
  are enforced at dispatch, while a store-owned approved-template catalog,
  outbound command idempotency, verified Instagram receipt ingestion, live
  design-partner acceptance, and launch-monitoring evidence remain pending.

### Commission workspace operating contract

- Target segment: owners, supervisors, and finance operators at independent
  used-vehicle dealerships with seller commission rules.
- Customer outcome: close a seller period without spreadsheets while tracing
  every amount back to the closed sale and vehicle snapshot.
- Leading metrics: percentage of closed sales with a complete commission state,
  reconciliation issue count, and time from period review to settlement.
- Billing and entitlement: remains part of the existing Finance surface; access
  is controlled separately by `finance.read` and `finance.update` permissions.
- Support owner: Finance (`api/web`), with production data incidents owned by
  the backend service owner and workflow/UI incidents by the web owner.
- Failure and degraded state: stale settlement input fails atomically with no
  partial payment; sales without commission, orphan links, standard-commission
  seller mismatch, missing vehicles, and cancelled/reverted sales remain
  visible as explicit reconciliation issues.

### Vehicle post studio operating contract

- Target segment: dealership owners, inventory operators, and social-media
  staff publishing vehicle offers from the active stock catalog.
- Customer outcome: turn an existing vehicle photo into an on-brand feed or
  story asset without leaving inventory or using an external design tool.
- Leading metrics: successful export rate, median time from studio open to
  first export, and percentage of active listings exported at least once.
- Billing and entitlement: part of stock control under `inventory.read`; the
  optional AI studio action retains its separate AI generation entitlement and
  quota.
- Support owner: Inventory (`api/web`), with media delivery and tenant-scope
  incidents owned by the backend owner and editor/export incidents by the web
  owner.
- Failure and degraded state: vehicles without photos show a guided empty state
  and disable exports; inaccessible or unsupported media surfaces a retryable
  preview error and never exports a silently photo-less asset; AI failure does
  not block the manual post editor.

## Commercial Launch Gate

A route, screen, adapter, or provider-readiness check is not enough to clear a
commercial launch. A feature is customer-ready only when all applicable items
below are evidenced:

1. At least one real design-partner store completed the workflow with its own
   data and accepted the outcome.
2. The workflow has an explicit activation or adoption metric and an internal
   owner can see failures without querying raw production data.
3. Billing, entitlement, quota, provider cost, cancellation, and support
   ownership are defined.
4. Tenant isolation, private-data storage, secrets, audit, idempotency, retry,
   and rollback behavior are proven for the risk class.
5. Required external contracts and Brazilian regulatory fields are confirmed
   against current primary sources.
6. The V1-to-V2 migration and rollback path exists when an active customer
   depends on equivalent V1 behavior.

Current P0 commercialization blockers are maintained in
[`strategy/product-operating-model.md`](strategy/product-operating-model.md).
