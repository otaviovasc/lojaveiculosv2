# CRM implementation review — 7 September 2026

The review started after pulling `feat/vehicle-vitrine-design` at
`a037e5ad8de9749e665bad40343544519deebc48`. The branch was current with its
upstream. V2 CRM flows were compared with the V1 CRM in `../repasses-loja`.

## Findings

| Priority      | Finding                                                                                  | Impact and correction                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P1            | Revoked connection membership did not reliably prevent a restricted seller from sending. | Enforce connection membership at the outbound boundary, including when the membership adapter is missing. Existing assignment does not restore revoked access. |
| P2            | A schedule edit could race a worker claim.                                               | Guard edits and worker transitions atomically, use the claimed payload, and fence completion against stale workers.                                            |
| P2            | A worker crash could leave a schedule in `sending` indefinitely.                         | Recover expired leases through the durable outbound intent. Confirmed sends are reconciled; unknown outcomes remain pending reconciliation with backoff.       |
| P2            | Paused campaign rows could occupy the entire due batch.                                  | Apply campaign eligibility before the database limit while retaining recovery of already-started deliveries.                                                   |
| P2            | Provider success followed by bookkeeping failure could be reported as delivery failure.  | Persist provider outcome separately, reconcile campaign bookkeeping independently, and keep delivery counters idempotent under concurrency.                    |
| P2            | A reply arriving before campaign bookkeeping recovered could be missed.                  | Admit recipients with a confirmed initial send and commit reply attribution, counters, and the follow-up schedule atomically.                                  |
| V1 parity gap | Campaigns lacked the image attachment workflow.                                          | Add managed image upload, preview/removal, strict input validation, durable scheduled media delivery, and attachment readback.                                 |
| V1 parity gap | Annual occasion automation was absent.                                                   | Add disabled-by-default connection settings for birthdays, purchase anniversaries, Easter, Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day, and Black Friday.            |

## Integration checks

The repair review also covers:

- Temporary provider rejections remaining retryable rather than becoming
  terminal scheduled-message failures.
- Revised automation schedules staying dormant until eligible, without
  blocking unrelated due messages or losing their annual execution identity.
- Revised automation after a known retryable failure retaining its annual
  execution identity while receiving a fresh scheduled-message idempotency key.
  Confirmed or uncertain deliveries remain on the reconciliation path.
- Explicit tenant/store scoping in the memory recipient adapter as well as
  the PostgreSQL implementation.
- Existing text-send idempotency identities across deployment, including replay
  after a connection becomes unavailable.
- Provider-confirmed deliveries followed by audit, realtime, or persistence
  failures, without an additional provider call.
- Campaign image cleanup only before a successful database commit; an audit
  response failure must not delete an asset referenced by a saved campaign.
- Image caption length after variable substitution, preserved drafts, and
  connection changes during asynchronous image reads.
- Canonical, nullable lead birth dates with strict calendar validation and
  Brazil-time date handling.
- Correct Black Friday, midnight, leap-day, and year-boundary calculations.
- Atomic automation revisions, annual recipient deduplication, cancellation,
  tenant-scoped references, and evaluation beyond the first page of stores.
- Completed sales as the source of purchase anniversaries.
- Shared UI controls, saved-versus-unsaved states, and desktop/mobile browser
  workflows using synthetic data.
